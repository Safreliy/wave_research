CMMP preparation check, 1 October 2026

Official journal guide:
https://www.pleiades.online/en/journal/commat/authors-instructions/

The guide specifies numbered square-bracket citations in order of first
appearance, a 150--250-word abstract, 4--6 keywords, informative captions,
and citations to every table in numerical order. It requests author
affiliation/location, email and available ORCID. Its text guidance specifies
10-point Times New Roman. The current TeX draft uses 10-point Times-compatible
fonts, 230 abstract words, six keywords, and numbered references. Author
city/country still need to be supplied; no location has been inferred.

Publisher LaTeX route:
https://www.pleiades.online/en/authors/guidlines/prepare-electonic-version/tex-latex/
This provides a legacy REVTeX 4 / MAIK package and examples for editorial
boards accepting TeX. The present editable article-class file remains the
review master, not a claimed CMMP production template. Confirm the portal's
accepted source format before final delivery; if required, prepare its Word
or MAIK submission version from this same scientific content. Do not replace
the current editor document merely to imitate a typeset journal article.

Preprints:
The CMMP guide says that the journal follows Springer Nature editorial
policies. Springer Nature's explicit preprint policy allows posting before
and during review and does not classify it as prior publication:
https://support.springernature.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000258807-preprints
No CMMP-specific preprint prohibition was found in the checked guide. This
supports keeping an author preprint online. Disclose its URL/DOI at submission.
This finding does not grant redistribution rights to the eventual publisher
PDF; that is a separate version and agreement. A code/data DOI is also distinct
from a manuscript preprint DOI. No manuscript or DOI deposit was submitted here.
