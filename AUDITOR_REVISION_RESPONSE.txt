Response to the external manuscript assessment
Revision date: 2 October 2026

This document records changes against the assessment supplied by the author.
It is a revision checklist, not a journal acceptance statement.

1. Central contribution and geometric scope
The introduction now distinguishes the general polygonal liquid-moment
construction from the Matched extension for horizontal flat surface strips.
Shared-edge cancellation is identified as a discrete Green identity. The
new accuracy mechanism is cancellation of the common physical trace in the
thin-strip edge difference. The overturning example remains a test of the
general construction, not a validation of the flat-strip extension.

2. Closest methods
A compact table compares conservative scalar remapping, Curl-Flow, an
embedded-boundary Stokes discretisation and the present construction by
input/output, preserved property and objective. It does not rank accuracy
between unrelated algorithms. The cited primary works support the entries.

3. Analysis of the implemented operator
Proposition 2 now bounds the actual blended edge operator, including the
term beta(d/h)|I_C-J(d)|/d. The normal-derivative errors, their sampling
contributions and the Taylor remainder are explicit. A conditional uniform
second-order implication is separated from the fixed-grid removal of the
1/d pole. The additional O(h^3) edge-compatibility assumption is not claimed
to follow from pointwise second-order accuracy of the harmonic solve.

4. Structure and terminology
The main results start with the final Matched operator, its mechanism
ablation, propagated transfer errors and independent physical comparison.
Detailed earlier protocols, unsuccessful variants and ranking reversals
remain in an appendix. Linear (L), Hermite (H), Matched (M) and Bilinear (B)
are consistent across text, tables and plots. A five-step procedure connects
geometry, reconstruction, edge integrals and receiver assignment.

5. Targeted experiments
The new campaign is recorded under transfer_auditor_revision_20261002.
All policies were fixed before the corresponding outcomes were analysed.
The fixed trace/grid-ratio initial comparison has three grids, 512x32,
1024x64 and 2048x128, and trace factors 8, 16 and 32. Matched global errors
are 1.4203093601e-3, 3.5377916132e-4 and 8.0414000273e-5; observed orders
are 2.0053 and 2.1373. The source boundary state remains fixed, so this is
not an upstream boundary-source convergence claim.
All nine dynamic branches on the corrected receiver completed to 0.1T.
The Bilinear-to-Matched endpoint velocity-error ratios are 7.882, 9.801 and
17.807; the surface-profile ratios are 65.019, 73.825 and 102.376. Matched
improves both metrics at every saved nonzero time on each grid. Relative
mass discrepancy remains below 7.15e-13. This native comparison retains
the explicitly declared 8/8/16 trace policy, distinct from the fixed-ratio
initial refinement test. Earlier unmodified-runtime and half-step results
remain available in the appendix rather than being silently replaced.
CPU timings now isolate trace preparation and in-memory transfer, excluding
serialization, checkpointing and CFD. Median transfer times on an Intel
Core Ultra 7 155H are 3.8285 s and 23.5884 s for the two tested grids;
the table reports all stage medians and ranges over three measured runs.
The two-grid independent physical comparison is complete, with all six
branches run on that same corrected runtime. Under identical mixture
assignment, Matched reduces endpoint physical profile error by 5.4896%
on 512x32 and 2.8123% on 1024x64. The full eight-row nonzero-time table
retains the first coarse-grid sample where Bilinear is better; Matched is
better at the other three coarse samples and all four fine samples. The
fine-grid errors are 0.0095706471 for exact input, 0.0095740968 for Matched
and 0.0098511410 for Bilinear. Physical refinement and propagated transfer
accuracy are kept distinct in the interpretation.

6. Reproducibility
The corrected Aphros runtime is tied to its saved build receipt and patched
source snapshot. The fresh source tree matches all 1605 recorded source
files byte for byte; the only absent archive member is a generated manifest.
Executable/library hashes are checked against the build record. The narrow
zero-displacement guard and the full source changes are retained separately.
This verification does not claim a new independent clean rebuild.
The original v0.31.0 tag, Zenodo record and frozen numerical archives are
preserved. New follow-up evidence is not attributed to that earlier DOI.

7. Deliberate scope limits
No 3D or curved-interface extension is introduced. A new analytic curved
boundary benchmark was optional in the assessment and is not needed for
the explicitly flat-strip accuracy claim. Existing curved-state tests and
their limits are retained. No complete breaking-wave convergence claim is
made. CMMP remains the intended venue; no submission has been made here.

8. Final checks
The new supplement contains 93 manifest-listed files and all 15 completed
native runs. Its standalone verifier checks 180 input files and successfully
recomputes all three principal numerical audits from the archived fields.
The evidence is fixed at repository commit e3ad0a9e0d5fa7c3399a23addb38874d345da5b2.
The publication PDF is built from the revised source with two pdfLaTeX
passes; the publication manifest and build receipt link source, tables,
figures and PDF by SHA-256. The original v0.31.0 release is unchanged.
